About FreeWeb Tools

Empowering developers with free, fast, and reliable online tools.

Our Mission

FreeWeb Tools was created with a simple mission: to provide developers, designers, and content creators with high-quality, free online tools that just work. We believe that essential development utilities should be accessible to everyone, without paywalls, signups, or watermarks.

What We Offer

We provide a growing collection of web-based tools designed to streamline your workflow:

  • JSON Formatter — Format, validate, and minify JSON data instantly
  • Base64 Encoder/Decoder — Convert text and data to and from Base64 encoding
  • CSS Box Shadow Generator — Create beautiful shadow effects visually
  • CSS Gradient Generator — Build stunning gradients with real-time preview
  • Markdown Editor — Write and preview Markdown in real time
  • HTML Entity Encoder — Encode and decode HTML special characters

Our Values

Privacy First: All processing happens in your browser. We never send your data to any server. Your code, text, and data stay on your machine.

Speed & Simplicity: Our tools are designed to be fast and distraction-free. No ads blocking your workflow, no unnecessary loading screens.

Always Free: Core tools will always be free to use. We sustain our operations through minimal, non-intrusive advertising.
